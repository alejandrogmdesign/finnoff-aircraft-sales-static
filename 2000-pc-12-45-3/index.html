An impressively-upgraded Pilatus PC-12/45, serial number 307, bearing U.S. registration mark N307WL, is for sale. The aircraft has recently completed the factory LEP phase 1 overhaul. New paint, new nine-place interior, new zero time upgraded engine (PT6A-67P), new 300 amp second generator with GCU, new zero time five-blade MT composite propeller, new WAAS-approved Garmin 650 and 750 touch-screen GPS Nav/Comms with Satellite Weather and XM “infotainment” package installed! Call soon to pick your paint scheme!

The aircraft is currently completing the Pilatus Life Extension Program Phase I (SB04-009) inspection, in which the aircraft is disassembled, all wear items are replaced with new components, critical components receive testing for suitability for return to service, and many other components of the airframe are replaced. The two-part LEP program utilizes factory engineers and results in a significant increase to the aircraft’s useful life, up to 50,000 hours total time when LEP1 and LEP2 are completed.
